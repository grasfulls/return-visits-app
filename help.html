Return Visits App

A plain-language guide for everyday use

Recommended for regular phone use: add Return Visits to your Home Screen and use that copy consistently. It opens like an app, but its contacts are still stored locally on this device. The browser version and Home Screen app may keep separate data and do not automatically sync; adding the icon does not transfer contacts already saved in your browser. If you already entered contacts in a browser, either continue using that browser or export an encrypted backup and restore it in the Home Screen app before switching. See backup instructions.

  • iPhone or iPad: For the best Home Screen app experience, open this page in Safari. If the link opened inside another app, use its menu or Share options to choose Open in Safari. In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open Return Visits from its new icon.
  • Android: Open this page in Chrome, open the browser menu, and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. Menu wording may vary by device.

A website cannot force links to open in Safari or install itself. Complete these steps before entering contacts when possible. The browser copy and Home Screen app may have separate local data; back up before switching if you already have contacts.

What this app does

Return Visits is free to use, with no app fees or subscription. It is intentionally a simple tool for maintaining contact details and tracking return visits, not a full-featured contact-management app. It may have fewer features or be less reliable than paid products. The app is provided as-is. Your internet or mobile provider may charge for data, and third-party services have their own terms.

The app helps you organize contact details and visit notes, find contacts by name or location, view contacts on a map, and print contact information.

Important: your information stays on this device

Contacts and settings are stored in the browser on the device where you use the app. They do not automatically sync to your other devices or browsers.

Before changing devices or browsers, clearing browser data, or removing and reinstalling the app, make an encrypted backup. Local records may become unavailable after these changes. To use your contacts again, restore the backup on the device where you reinstall or open the app. Step-by-step instructions are in Protect your information and make a backup.

If you have contacts in more than one browser or app copy, choose one copy to keep before deleting anything. Full backups replace the destination data; they do not merge copies. To combine several collections, use one copy as the base and import individual contacts from the other copies. Verify the combined records, then make a fresh backup before clearing unused copies. See Consolidate copies and remove unused data.

Passphrase (optional)

  • A passphrase is not required. By default the app opens right away and stores your contacts on this device without encryption.
  • To protect your data, open Settings → Turn On Passphrase and choose a passphrase with at least 8 characters. Your saved data is then encrypted, and you will enter the passphrase each time you open the app on that device and browser.
  • If you turn a passphrase on, keep it somewhere safe. There is no passphrase recovery. If you forget it, the app cannot unlock that device's encrypted contacts.
  • A passphrase does not sync or restore contacts by itself. To recover your contacts after reinstalling the app or to move them to another device, you need a backup. See Protect your information and make a backup.

Change your passphrase

If a passphrase is on, after unlocking this copy of the app, open Settings, enter your current passphrase, then enter and confirm a new passphrase of at least 8 characters. The app re-encrypts this copy's saved contacts and settings with the new passphrase. This does not change other browser or Home Screen copies, or the passphrase on backup files you already exported. Keep using the old passphrase for those existing backups; a new backup will use the new passphrase.

Add and organize contacts

  • Choose Add Contact, enter a first name, and add any other details you have. A first name is required; other contact details can be completed later.
  • Email and phone are optional. Enter an email such as name@someemail. Phone numbers are formatted as (555) 123-4567; enter a 10-digit U.S. number, or include a leading 1 for a U.S. country code.
  • A contact can have one City, one Region within that City, and one Area within that Region. Region becomes available after choosing a City; Area becomes available after choosing a Region. These location details are optional. The Area list includes + Add New Area... even when other areas are available, so you can create an area for the selected City and Region without leaving the contact form. The Region list likewise includes + Add New Region... for the selected City.
  • In Settings, use Edit beside a City, Region, or Area to rename it; existing contact assignments and child locations are updated. Delete is blocked while any contact or child location still uses the entry. Reassign or remove those first, then delete the unused location.
  • Different contacts may have the same name. The app assigns each contact a permanent ID to tell them apart.
  • Choose Save to keep your changes. To edit an existing contact, use the pencil/edit action on its contact card.
  • If you choose Exit or close the editor after making changes, the app asks whether to leave without saving. Confirming discards those changes; canceling keeps the editor open.
  • Use the Map button in the bottom navigation to view contacts that match the current search and filters and have map coordinates. Changing the search or filters also updates the map. Contacts without coordinates do not have a pin on the map. If no visible contacts have coordinates, the map centers approximately on the Default City and State saved in Settings. While the Map is open without pins, that city and state are sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim for the lookup.

In the contact form, choose Find Address or City Pin to look up a location using OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Choose Use Device Location instead to request the phone's current location. GPS uses the device's location, not the contact's address, and its accuracy can vary. The form shows the GPS-reported accuracy when available. Typing or saving an address does not run a lookup or create a pin automatically. Street addresses and newly entered location names are capitalized when you leave the field or save. Recognized U.S. state names are saved as standard two-letter abbreviations, and state codes are uppercased.

  • If a partial street address has multiple possible matches, choose the correct street from the street-name list; choices omit house numbers and ZIP codes. Selecting a match fills in the street name using abbreviations such as Av and Ct, and places a pin. Contact addresses use abbreviated street suffixes throughout the app, including contacts saved earlier (for example, Avenue as Av and Road as Rd). If the full address is not found, the app also searches by the street name without its leading house number. You can choose a city without entering a street to place an approximate city pin. If you need a more precise result, enter a nearby cross-street or intersection and search again. These searches send the entered location text to OpenStreetMap Nominatim only after you choose the lookup button.
  • Contacts saved without coordinates have no map pin. The Map page reports when matching contacts need coordinates; reopen one and use Find Address or City Pin to add them.
  • Both methods place a pin that you can refine. Tap another point on the contact form map, drag the pin, or edit the latitude and longitude fields. You can do this while adding or editing a contact. You can also drag an existing contact's pin on the Map page; its new coordinates save automatically.
  • Check that the pin is in the right place before choosing Save. Address lookup may not identify the exact entrance or building; device location may also need manual adjustment.

Map tiles also need an internet connection. Coordinates are optional, so you can save a contact without a pin and add coordinates later.

Find contacts and record visits

  • Use the search box to search names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, or visit notes.
  • Narrow the list with urgency, City, Region, or Area filters. Sort by newest, oldest, or name. Clear filters to see the full contact list again.
  • Choose 🧭 Navigate on a contact card to open Google Maps directions. The app uses saved coordinates when available; otherwise it uses the contact's address. The destination is sent to Google Maps, and you can use the navigation options available on your device.
  • Choose Share on a contact card, then choose Text / SMS, WhatsApp, or Email. The selected app opens with a plain-text summary containing the contact's name and any available phone, email, address, and directions link. Visit notes are not included. This is plain text, not an encrypted contact export. Review the message before sending; your chosen messaging or email service receives the details. For encrypted app-to-app transfer, use the contact's export action instead.
  • Add a visit note from the contact card or while editing a contact. On the contact card, type the note into the "Add visit log..." box and choose the plus button to save it with today's date and time. In the contact editor, enter a note and choose Save; you can adjust its date and time there. Existing notes can be edited or deleted from the contact editor.
  • Urgency is based on the most recent visit (or the contact's creation date if there are no visit notes): Normal is 0-7 days, Urgent is 8-14 days, and Immediate is 15 or more days.

Print contacts

  • On the contact list, choose Print Visible Contacts to print the contacts currently shown. Search terms and filters determine what is included. The app confirms the number of contacts before printing.
  • A list print includes each contact once, grouped under its most specific location: Area, or Region if there is no Area, or City if there is no Region or Area. Contacts without any of these are grouped under "No location assigned." Each contact's list entry includes its available phone and email details and only its latest note.
  • To print one contact, open that saved contact and choose Print. Choose the most recent note only (the default) or all notes.
  • Save contact edits before printing. If the Print button is unavailable while editing, save your changes first. Printing opens your device or browser's print options; saving as a PDF may be available there.

Protect your information and make a backup

Your contacts and settings are stored in this device's browser, and are encrypted if you have turned on a passphrase. They are not uploaded to an account and are not automatically backed up. Clearing browser data, losing the device, or forgetting the passphrase (if one is on) can make the local records unavailable. Make backups regularly and keep them somewhere safe.

  • In Settings, choose Export Data Backup. The backup includes all contacts and settings, is encrypted if a passphrase is on, and is downloaded by your browser to its usual Downloads location.
  • To move your full collection to a new device, open the app there and choose Choose Backup to Restore on the first screen. Select the backup and, if it is encrypted, enter the passphrase used when it was created.
  • Restoring a full backup replaces the contacts and settings currently on that device. Check the confirmation carefully before continuing.
  • To share one contact, use that contact card's export/download action. This creates an encrypted contact file. Give the recipient the file and its separate sharing passphrase through different channels. Import it from Settings; it adds that contact without replacing the recipient's other contacts.

Consolidate copies and remove unused data

If Safari, Chrome, and a Home Screen app each have contacts, they are separate local copies and do not sync. For regular phone use, choose the Home Screen app as the one copy to keep. A full backup restore replaces all contacts and settings in the destination; it does not merge two full backups.

  1. Decide which copy will be your permanent one. If it already contains contacts you want to keep, leave it as the destination and import other contacts into it individually. If you choose a different base copy, remember that restoring its full backup will replace the destination's current contacts and settings.
  2. Before changing anything, export an encrypted full backup from every copy that contains information you may need. Keep these backups until the consolidation is complete.
  3. Use the full backup from your chosen base copy to populate the destination, if needed. From every other copy, export contacts one at a time using each contact card's download/export button, then import each encrypted contact file into the destination from Settings → Share a Contact. Individual contact imports add records; they do not replace the collection. This can take time if a source contains many contacts.
  4. Review the destination for missing or duplicate contacts and confirm important visit notes are present. Contact imports may create a duplicate if the same person is already in the destination.
  5. Export a new encrypted full backup from the destination. Keep it somewhere safe and confirm you know its passphrase before deleting any source copy.
  6. Only after verification, remove the unused copies using the removal instructions below. Clear data only for a source copy you no longer need. If the browser's controls do not make it clear which copy will be removed, do not clear it until you have confirmed the surviving copy still opens with all your contacts.

Full backups cannot be merged directly. If you need to bring over contacts from a second full backup, restore that backup only if you are ready to replace the destination, or import its contacts individually. Do not delete source data or backup files until the destination has been checked and backed up.

Stop using the app and remove its saved data

Remove unused app data

Uninstalling the Home Screen app alone may not remove its browser-stored data. To remove the app's local contacts and settings, clear the site data for grasfulls.github.io in the browser that you used. This permanently deletes that browser copy. If you want to keep your contacts, export an encrypted backup first.

  • iPhone or iPad: To remove the Safari browser copy, open iOS Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data, find grasfulls.github.io, and delete its website data. To remove a separate Home Screen app copy, touch and hold its icon, choose Remove App, then Delete App. Menu names may vary slightly by iOS version.
  • Android (Chrome): Open Chrome's settings for grasfulls.github.io and choose Clear data or Clear & reset. You can also find it under Settings → Site settings → All sites.
  • Other browsers or computers: Use the browser's website data or site storage settings to remove data for grasfulls.github.io.
  • Repeat this in each browser and on each device where you used the app; each copy may have separate data. Uninstall the Home Screen app separately if you also want to remove its icon.
  • This does not delete downloaded backups, exported contact files, PDFs, or printed copies. Remove those separately from Downloads, Files, cloud storage, or wherever you saved or shared them.

Use the app on another device

On another device, open the Return Visits app in a web browser. Your contacts will not appear automatically; restore an encrypted backup using the instructions above. For regular use on a phone, we recommend adding the app to your Home Screen: in Safari on iPhone, use Share then Add to Home Screen; in Chrome on Android, choose Install app or Add to Home screen. The app checks for updates when it opens and prompts you once it has opened if an update is ready; an exclamation mark also appears by Settings. To check or refresh manually, open Settings → Check for Updates & Refresh while online. Save or finish any open contact or Settings edits first. The app reloads its code without removing the contacts and settings stored in that app copy. After it reloads, unlock using the same passphrase if you have turned one on.

Already using the app in your phone's browser?

The browser version and the Home Screen app do not automatically sync their contacts. Adding the app to your Home Screen does not transfer the data already saved in your browser; the two versions may keep separate local copies. To move your contacts, first confirm they are visible in the browser version, then choose Settings → Export Data Backup. Open the Home Screen app and choose Choose Backup to Restore on its unlock screen, or use its restore option in Settings. Keep using the browser copy until you have confirmed the contacts are visible in the Home Screen app. After the transfer, changes made in one copy will not appear in the other; export and restore a fresh backup when you want to move newer changes.

The app is free to use, but it does not include an online sync service. If you use more than one copy, keep track of which one has your latest changes. You must manually export and restore encrypted backups to transfer changes; restoring a full backup replaces the destination copy's contacts and settings.
