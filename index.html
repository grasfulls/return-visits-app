Cities, Regions & Areas

Add New City to List

How locations work: A city is divided into regions, which are large parts of the city (such as North, South, East, and West). A region is divided into areas, which are smaller neighborhoods within that region. New cities are automatically given the regions North, South, East, and West; you can add more or delete any you don't need.

Add New Region

You can also add a region while adding a contact. Choose + Add New Region... in that contact's Region list to add one under the selected City.

Add New Area

Manage Cities, Regions, and Areas

Edit renames a location and updates its existing references. Delete is blocked while any contact is assigned to it or to a location beneath it. Deleting a city or region with no assigned contacts also deletes the regions and areas under it, after you confirm. Locations follow City → Region → Area; each level is optional on a contact.
