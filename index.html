Cities, Regions & Areas

Add New City to List

How locations work: A city is divided into regions, which are large parts of the city (such as North, South, East, and West). A region is divided into areas, which are smaller neighborhoods within that region. Cities, regions, and areas are added as you enter them for yourself or a contact; use each field's + Add New choice to add one.

Add New Region

You can also add a region while adding a contact. Choose + Add New Region... in that contact's Region list to add one under the selected City.

Manage Cities, Regions, and Areas

Deleting a city or region with no assigned contacts also deletes the regions and areas under it, after you confirm. Locations follow City → Region → Area; each level is optional on a contact.

Cloud Backup (optional)

Cloud backup keeps a copy of your contacts outside this device, in your own Dropbox, so you can recover them if your device is lost, broken or replaced. It is off unless you turn it on. Dropbox is entirely optional: the app works fully without it, and all your data can stay only on this device.

You need a Dropbox account. The app can send you to Dropbox to sign up, but it cannot create the account for you. The free Basic plan is enough.

Do you already have a Dropbox account?

Create a free Dropbox account

  1. Open dropbox.com/basic (it opens in a new tab or window).
  2. Sign up with your email address (or your Google or Apple account) and choose a password.
  3. Choose the free Basic plan. If Dropbox offers a paid trial, skip it. No payment is needed.
  4. Check your email and confirm your address if Dropbox asks.
  5. Come back to this app and tap the button below.

Connect your Dropbox

  1. Tap Connect to Dropbox below.
  2. Sign in to Dropbox when it asks.
  3. Tap Allow. The app can only use its own folder, called Return Visits, and cannot see anything else in your Dropbox.
  4. Dropbox sends you back here.

The app backs up when you open it if your last backup is more than a day old, and shortly after you make changes. Dropbox keeps your latest copy plus up to 10 dated copies.

Using more than one device? Each device combines its changes with Dropbox automatically: when you open the app, every couple of minutes while it is open, and as soon as the connection returns after being offline. Visits and contacts added on different devices are all kept. If you have no signal, your changes stay saved on the device and upload as soon as it can reach Dropbox. The cloud icon at the top shows the state (tap it to sync now). You can also tap Check for a Newer Backup.

If two devices change the very same contact at the same time, the most recent change to that contact is used, but visits from both devices are kept.

Passphrase

Turn Off Passphrase?

If you turn the passphrase off:

  • The contacts and settings on this device are decrypted and stored without encryption. Anyone who can open this browser or app on this device could read them.
  • The app opens right away and no longer asks for a passphrase.
  • Your recovery code stops working, because there is no passphrase left to recover.
  • Backups you make from now on, including Dropbox backups, are not encrypted. Backups you already made keep the passphrase they were created with.
  • You can turn a passphrase on again at any time.

Recovery Code

A recovery code is a long random code you keep somewhere safe, away from this device. If you forget your passphrase, type the code in place of the passphrase to open the app. Anyone with the code can open your data, so treat it like the passphrase.
